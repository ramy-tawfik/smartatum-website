02 · Website, cookies, and playtests

Information connected to the website

Website delivery, security, and cookies

When you visit smartatum.com, our hosting and security provider (Cloudflare) may process ordinary network and request information such as IP address, browser or user-agent information, requested URL, date and time, and security signals. This information is used to deliver the site, prevent abuse, and maintain reliability.

Smart Atum Studio does not use smartatum.com to serve behavioral advertising, does not sell personal information, and does not set advertising or analytics cookies on the website. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary security mechanisms or cookies to protect the service, and promotional pages such as the closed-alpha and promo-claim pages load Google Fonts, which sends ordinary request metadata such as your IP address to Google. External websites linked from our pages are governed by their own policies.

Promo-code redemptions

If you visit our promo-claim page (smartatum.com/claim) and submit a claim code, that code is sent over HTTPS to a Smart Atum Cloudflare Worker (promoclaim.smartatum.workers.dev) solely to validate one-time eligibility and return your reward code.

Closed-playtest applications and Discord

Hero Investor PC playtest applications are hosted directly on Google Forms. The application may ask for your Discord username, country or time zone, Windows and hardware specifications, display configuration, testing availability, feedback interests, relevant game experience, and written responses.

We use application responses only to evaluate applicants, coordinate testing, communicate with selected participants through our private Discord test channel, protect the test, and improve Hero Investor PC. Applying does not require a purchase. Google and Discord separately process use of Google Forms and Discord under their own privacy policies.

03 · Hero Investor for Windows PC

PC data stays local unless you choose otherwise

Files stored on the PC

Hero Investor PC may store saves, autosaves, manual checkpoints, settings, logs, screenshots created by the game, pending bug-report packages, and bounded gameplay diagnostics in the game’s Windows application-data folders. These files remain on the PC unless you use an explicit transfer feature.

Machine-local diagnostics are limited in size and rotation. A random installation identifier may be stored locally to connect diagnostic events from the same installation; it is not your Steam identity. The game provides a separate control to clear local analytics diagnostics without deleting saves or ordinary settings.

Local gameplay diagnostics stay on your PC. A separate optional setting controls automatic Sentry crash reports and anonymous gameplay counters in configured builds.

Steam features

When supported and enabled in the build you use, the game may ask the Steam client to synchronize eligible save archives, unlock achievements, update achievement progress statistics, confirm ownership, display the Steam overlay, or show broad rich-presence information such as general gameplay state, in-game year, or company level.

Machine-specific settings, live databases, logs, local diagnostics, and pending bug-report packages are not intended for Steam Cloud synchronization. Valve processes Steam account, purchase, Cloud, achievement, platform, and usage information under Valve’s own agreements and privacy policy.

Optional crash reporting and anonymous gameplay counters

Smart Atum Studio uses Sentry in configured Hero Investor for Windows PC builds (including playtest, demo, beta, and release builds) to diagnose crashes and selected errors and to measure anonymous gameplay balance. On first launch, you can choose whether to enable optional reporting (in closed-playtest builds the checkbox may start pre-selected before you confirm; in public release builds it starts unchecked). Choose Agree and enable reports to turn it on, or clear the box and choose Agree and continue to play with reporting off. You can change your choice in Settings at any time. Turning it off stops future reports.

When enabled in a configured build, the game automatically sends technical error reports and anonymous gameplay counters and milestone events to Sentry in the United States. Error reports may include the exception type and stack frames, thread, game version, system and runtime details, current and previous screens, and game phase. Anonymous gameplay telemetry is limited to closed event, screen, and action categories (such as screen visits, day advances, trades by asset class, upgrades, and onboarding or level/year milestones) with no personal identifiers or financial amounts. Where enabled in a build, optional reports may also include low-rate sampled technical performance timings (such as startup, navigation, day-advance, and save/restore durations). Automatic reports exclude saves, screenshots, application logs, text you enter, Steam identity, account names, and financial values.

The Sentry project disables IP address storage. Event retention follows the project’s configured Sentry retention period. For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the data-deletion page or email contact@smartatum.com.

Optional in-game bug reports

Manual bug reports are separate from automatic Sentry crash reports. If an online reporting service is enabled, a report is transferred only after you open Report a Bug, complete the form, choose any optional diagnostic items, and press the send button.

An approved report may include your written issue description, reproduction steps, application and data-format versions, coarse operating-system and hardware details, and—only when you approve them—recent sanitized logs or one screenshot captured by the game.

The reporting flow does not intentionally include save archives, active databases, preferences, Steam identity, account credentials, browser data, arbitrary file attachments, unrelated files, or a screenshot you did not approve. If no production endpoint is available or sending fails, a pending package may remain locally for an explicit retry.

05 · How we use information and legal bases

Limited to operating and improving our services

Depending on the service and applicable law, Smart Atum Studio uses information to:

  • provide, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the website, promo-code redemptions, and games;
  • evaluate and coordinate playtests and communicate with participants;
  • diagnose crashes, defects, compatibility problems, and performance issues, and evaluate anonymous gameplay balance in opted-in PC builds;
  • protect private tests and services from fraud, spam, or abuse;
  • respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and protect legal rights.

We do not use information submitted directly to Smart Atum Studio for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

Legal bases for processing

Where a legal basis is required under applicable law (such as in the EEA or the United Kingdom), processing depends on the feature and context: consent (for example, optional PC crash and gameplay reporting or regional advertising consent), providing requested features or performing a contract (such as delivering game features, promo-code redemptions, or purchases), legitimate interests (such as website security, service reliability, crash troubleshooting, fraud and abuse prevention, and responding to inquiries, where not overridden by your rights), and compliance with legal obligations. This policy does not replace an affirmative consent choice where one is required.

06 · Sharing, service providers, and international transfers

We do not sell personal information

We do not sell your personal information. In Android games that use third-party advertising mediation, regional consent choices and device ad settings govern whether advertising identifiers are shared for personalized advertising, as detailed in each game’s policy.

We may provide limited information to service providers only when necessary to operate, secure, or support the relevant service. Providers depend on the title and feature:

  • Cloudflare for website delivery, security, promo-code verification, and any separately enabled private bug-report infrastructure;
  • Google for Google Forms, Google Fonts on select promotional pages, Google Play, Firebase, AdMob, and Google Cloud services used by a particular title;
  • Android advertising and mediation partners (such as Unity and Meta, and additional networks where listed) as described in each Android game’s dedicated policy;
  • Sentry for optional crash/error reporting, anonymous gameplay counters, and (where enabled) sampled performance timings in configured Hero Investor for Windows PC builds;
  • Valve for Steam distribution and enabled Steam platform features; and
  • Discord for closed-playtest coordination and community channels you choose to join.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to investigate abuse or security incidents, or to protect users, Smart Atum Studio, or others. If the studio or its assets are reorganized or transferred, relevant information may transfer subject to this policy and applicable law.

International data transfers

Smart Atum Studio and its service providers (including infrastructure located in the United States and other countries) may process information outside your country of residence. Where required by law, we rely on provider safeguards and other lawful transfer mechanisms for cross-border processing.

07 · Retention and deletion

Kept only as long as needed

  • Local PC and device data remains on your device until you delete it through the game, system settings, or uninstall and cleanup processes.
  • Hero Investor PC optional Sentry reports (crash/error reports, anonymous gameplay counters, and any enabled performance timings) are retained according to the project’s configured Sentry retention period. Turning reporting off in Settings stops future reports.
  • Playtest applications and support communications are retained while reasonably necessary to evaluate applicants, operate the test, respond to the communication, handle disputes, and meet legal obligations.
  • Promo-code redemptions and online bug reports: promo-claim state is kept to prevent duplicate redemptions; if online bug-report submission is enabled, reports follow the retention period disclosed in the reporting flow and the version of this policy then in effect.
  • Platform and provider information is retained according to the relevant service settings, lifecycle, and policies. For Android analytics, purchase-verification, and security records, see the game-specific notice.

Uninstalling a game does not automatically remove cloud saves or previously collected online records. Our data-deletion page explains local, platform, and studio-managed data separately. Information may be deleted, aggregated, or anonymized when it is no longer needed. Some records may be retained longer where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

08 · Your choices and privacy rights

Controls and rights available to you

Depending on the service you use, you can:

  • decline to submit a playtest application, promo claim, or support request;
  • turn optional PC Sentry reporting (crash reports and anonymous gameplay counters) on or off in Settings;
  • decline optional logs or screenshots in an in-game bug report;
  • clear local PC diagnostics through the game’s Settings screen;
  • delete local saves and other files using the game’s documented controls or device/Windows settings;
  • manage eligible Steam Cloud data and Steam privacy choices through Steam;
  • manage Play Games data through Google and available Android advertising, consent, or notification choices through the game and device settings; and
  • exercise applicable privacy rights regarding information held directly by Smart Atum Studio.

Regional privacy rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, the United Kingdom, and applicable US states), you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a portable copy of personal data; object to or restrict certain processing; opt out of targeted advertising or sharing where applicable; withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal); and lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

Requests concerning Steam, Google, Discord, or another platform’s account data should be directed to that provider. For Smart Atum Studio requests, use our data-deletion page or email contact@smartatum.com. We may request limited information needed to locate the relevant record and verify that the request is legitimate, and we respond within the period required by applicable law.

09 · Security

Safeguards for your data

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and service involved, including encrypted HTTPS transport for website pages, promo-code redemptions, optional Sentry telemetry, and backend requests. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

10 · Children's privacy and age requirements

Age boundaries

The Hero Investor PC closed-playtest application is limited to people aged 18 or older. Our website, PC services, and games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A higher minimum age may apply where required by local law.

Supported Android titles apply age-appropriate session and advertising protections described in their game-specific notices. If you are a parent or guardian and believe personal information was collected from a child contrary to these requirements, email contact@smartatum.com so we can investigate and take reasonable steps to delete it.

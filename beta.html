The testing mandate

Play like an investor. Think like a developer.

This is an active development build, not a polished demo. We are looking for thoughtful players who can explore the systems, notice friction, and explain what happened clearly.

01

Stress the strategy

Build companies, trade assets, research upgrades, manage property, and look for choices that feel unclear or unbalanced.

02

Challenge the interface

Test different resolutions and display scaling. Report clipped text, confusing flows, input problems, and anything that breaks immersion.

03

Send useful evidence

When something goes wrong, share the steps, a screenshot or recording when possible, and the logs requested by the test team.

Candidate profile

You may be a good fit if…

  • You enjoy strategy, tycoon, finance, or management games.
  • You can test on a Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC.
  • You are comfortable joining the test Discord channel.
  • You can describe bugs respectfully and with useful detail.
  • You understand that saves and features may change during beta.

Before you apply

The important details.

Is the beta free?

Yes. There is no charge to apply or participate in this private development test.

Do I need the Android game?

No. The PC version is being developed as its own Windows experience, and new testers can start fresh.

Will my progress be permanent?

Not necessarily. Test saves may be reset or become incompatible as systems change during development.

How will I hear back?

Selected testers will be contacted through the Discord information provided in the application.

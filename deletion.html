Data Deletion

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Smart Atum Studio is an independent game studio operated by Ramy Tawfik. This page explains how to remove local information, manage platform saves, and request deletion of information held by the studio. The relevant steps depend on the game and services you use.

Uninstalling a game or resetting progress does not automatically delete its cloud saves, platform profile, analytics, purchase records, or information already shared with another app.

1. Local game data and exported files

For Android, use your device's app settings to clear the game's storage, or uninstall it. Clearing storage can permanently remove local progress and settings. Cloud data may be restored when you sign in or load a cloud save again.

Delete exported magazine images, shared reports, screenshots, or backup files separately from the locations where you saved them. If you shared content through another app, manage that copy through the receiving app.

For Windows PC games, remove the relevant saves, settings, or diagnostics using the game's available controls or its documented local file locations. Uninstalling may leave saved files behind. If you enabled a platform cloud-save service, manage that copy separately.

2. Google Play Games data

For games that use Play Games, including Hero Investor for Android, Google provides controls for deleting data for an individual game. In your Play Games profile, open Your Data, then the option to delete Play Games account and data. Under Delete individual game data, select the relevant game.

Menu names can vary. Follow Google's current deletion instructions for web and Android. This can affect saved progress, achievements, or scores. It does not delete separate data held by the developer or other services, or your Google account itself.

3. Request deletion from Smart Atum Studio

Email: smartatum@gmail.com
Suggested subject: [Game name] — Data deletion request

Please tell us the game or website feature involved and what you want deleted, such as support correspondence, identifiable analytics or diagnostic records, or purchase-verification records. You do not need to create a new studio account to submit a request.

We may request limited information needed to locate relevant records and confirm that they belong to you. Some services use installation or platform identifiers rather than an email address, so email alone may not identify every record. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details, or purchase tokens.

We will review the request, explain any information needed, and respond within the period required by applicable law. We will delete data we can identify and control where required, use applicable provider deletion procedures, or explain any exception or provider action needed.

4. Records that may need to be retained

Some purchase, fraud-prevention, security, or legal records may need to remain for a legitimate purpose or applicable obligation. Retention depends on the record, service settings, outstanding issue, and applicable requirements. If an exception applies to your request, we will explain it. Aggregated information that no longer identifies you may remain in statistical reports.

A deletion request does not itself refund a purchase, cancel a subscription where a game offers one, or erase Google's transaction records. Manage purchases and any subscriptions through the relevant store.

Privacy Policy for Dev Tycoon

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy applies to the Android game Dev Tycoon: Game Dev Simulator (package com.smartatum.devtycoon), including earlier versions named Dev Tycoon or Software Studio. Smart Atum Studio is an independent studio operated by Ramy Tawfik (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions, contact smartatum@gmail.com.

At a glance

  • Game progress and preferences are stored on your device. Android backup or device-transfer features may also copy eligible app data, depending on your device and settings.
  • The game uses Google AdMob and Unity Ads for advertising, and Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config for usage measurement, diagnostics, and app configuration.
  • Purchases use Google Play Billing and a purchase-verification service hosted on Google Cloud Run.
  • Support messages and sharing are initiated by you. Local reminders can be disabled in the game settings or Android notification settings.
  • Deleting the app or resetting your game does not automatically delete information already processed by online services.
  • Google Play Games integration is planned but is not currently available.

1. Game data on your device

The game stores progress, in-game balances, skills, products, contracts, achievements, preferences, and purchase entitlements using local storage, including SQLite and Android preferences. Financial figures inside the simulation are fictional gameplay values, not your real bank balances or financial accounts.

Local saves are separate from the gameplay events and diagnostic information processed by the online services described below. The presence of local saves does not mean that all information stays on your device. Android backup and device transfer are also separate from the planned Google Play Games cloud-save feature.

2. Analytics, diagnostics, and configuration

Firebase Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used and improve gameplay and monetization. Logged events include product development and release, contracts, skill training, in-game economic actions, tutorial progress, achievements, game resets, opening community links, ad outcomes, and purchase-flow outcomes. Event parameters can include gameplay amounts, scores, product categories, purchase product identifiers, and network availability. Player properties include progress groups and whether Pro or a Season Pass is active.

The service also processes app-instance identifiers, app and device information, and usage information according to its configuration. These records are not necessarily anonymous simply because they do not contain your name or email address.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and stability issues. It processes crash traces, installation identifiers, device and app information, and diagnostic context. The game adds context such as its lifecycle, advertising and billing states, monetization screen, and app and SDK versions.

Firebase Remote Config

We use Remote Config to retrieve configuration values, including the minimum supported app version for update prompts. This service processes installation identifiers and related technical information needed to deliver configuration.

More information: Firebase privacy and security and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

The game integrates Google AdMob and Unity Ads through AdMob mediation, including rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Advertising services may process advertising and installation identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize advertising.

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request applicable advertising privacy choices before initializing ads when the SDK permits ad requests. Available consent signals are forwarded to Unity. Choices shown can vary by region and the advertising provider's requirements.

You can use the advertising identifier controls available in Android settings to reset or delete your advertising ID, depending on your Android version. You may also use the choices offered by the advertising providers. Limiting personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads or stop every form of technical data processing.

Advertising choices do not automatically turn off Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Remote Config. Contact us if you need assistance with a privacy request.

Provider information: Google advertising technologies and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

4. Purchases and verification

Optional in-app purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment process; the game does not ask you to enter payment-card details into a studio-operated payment form.

To verify eligible purchases and grant or restore purchased content, the game sends the purchase token, product identifier, and app package name over HTTPS to our verification service hosted on Google Cloud Run. Purchase entitlements and fulfillment records are also stored locally to support access and prevent duplicate rewards.

Purchase-related data is used to validate transactions, deliver content, handle support, and prevent misuse. The game separately records limited purchase-flow outcomes in Analytics; these purchase-flow events do not attach purchase tokens or order IDs. Hosting infrastructure may process technical request metadata such as IP addresses and request times.

Uninstalling the game or requesting data deletion does not automatically refund purchases or cancel any recurring payments managed through Google Play.

5. Local notifications

The game schedules reminders on your device, such as energy reminders, inactivity reminders, and weekly summaries. These reminders use local Android scheduling and notification features, rather than Firebase Cloud Messaging in the current implementation.

You can disable reminders in the game's notification settings or in Android's notification settings. Android may also request notification permission, depending on your system version.

6. Support, community links, and sharing

If you choose to send a support email or bug report, we receive the message, your email address, and any information you include. The game's email actions can prefill the game version and locally stored game-state or preference information; the bug-report action also includes the Android version and device model. The game opens an external email app so you can review or edit the message before sending it.

We use this information to investigate issues and respond to you. Please review the prefilled information and do not include passwords, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Sharing features let you choose another app to share game-related content, such as a text message celebrating a fan milestone. You choose whether and where to send it. The receiving app handles the content under its own privacy policy.

Community links, including Discord, open third-party services. Information you choose to post or send there is handled by those services and may be visible to other community members. Opening a community link is also recorded as an in-game Analytics event.

7. Who processes information

Information is processed by Smart Atum Studio and the service providers described above: Google for Firebase, AdMob, Play Billing, and Cloud Run, and Unity for mediated advertising. Your selected email, sharing, or community services process information when you use them.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to a valid legal request, protect rights and security, or investigate fraud. Google, Unity, and other providers may process information in countries outside your country of residence, as described in their policies.

8. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the type of information and why it is needed. Relevant criteria include maintaining purchased entitlements, resolving support requests, diagnosing issues, preventing fraud, and meeting applicable legal obligations. Provider-held information is also subject to the service's retention settings and deletion processes. Analytics retention varies by the configured retention period and data category; aggregated reports may be retained separately. You can contact us for information about the retention applicable to a specific request.

  • Local progress: Reset Game starts a fresh game and removes relevant local progress. It does not necessarily remove every preference or purchase record. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app storage, but Android backups, transferred copies, or content you exported may remain separately.
  • Online information: Resetting or uninstalling the game does not automatically request deletion from Firebase, advertising providers, our purchase-verification service, or support email.
  • Deletion requests: See our data-deletion page or email smartatum@gmail.com with “Dev Tycoon — Data deletion” and describe the information you want deleted.

We may ask for limited information needed to identify the relevant records and verify your request. An email address alone may not identify records associated with an app installation. Do not send passwords, full payment details, or purchase tokens by email.

Where applicable, information may need to be retained for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or resolving purchase disputes. Provider backups and deletion processing may take additional time. We respond to requests within the time required by applicable law.

9. Your rights and legal bases

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain uses for targeted advertising or sale/sharing as defined by applicable law. Contact smartatum@gmail.com to make a request. You may also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority.

Where a legal basis is required, processing may be based on providing the game features or purchases you request, legitimate interests such as maintaining security, diagnosing faults and responding to support requests where permitted, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where required. Simply reading this policy or using the game is not a substitute for an affirmative consent choice where one is required. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing based on that consent.

10. Children's privacy

Children's information requires special care. A content rating is not itself a statement about data collection or parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information that should not have been collected, contact smartatum@gmail.com so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

We do not use this policy as parental authorization. Where applicable law requires parental consent or other protections, those requirements apply independently of this notice.

11. Security

The app uses Android app-storage protections, and purchase-verification requests use HTTPS. Firebase and advertising providers apply their own security measures, described in their documentation. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Please keep your device protected and avoid sharing sensitive information in public community channels.

12. Planned Google Play Games integration

We plan to add Google Play Games support for cloud saves, achievements, and leaderboards in a future Dev Tycoon update. This integration is not currently available, and Dev Tycoon does not currently collect or share data through Google Play Games.

Before this integration becomes available, we will update this policy to explain the features implemented, the data they use, and the choices available to players.

We will update this page when the game's data practices change and revise the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before a material change takes effect.

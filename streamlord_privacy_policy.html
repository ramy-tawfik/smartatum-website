Streamlord Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Smart Atum Studio is an independent game studio operated by Ramy Tawfik ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy describes how information is handled in Streamlord for Android, package com.smartatum.streamlord.

Streamlord is an offline-capable media-business entertainment simulation. Founder names, studio names, talent rosters, revenues, and financial figures created in gameplay are fictional in-game content, not real-world personal or financial records. All in-game characters, studios, competitor brands, logos, titles, and financial figures are fictional; see our Terms of Use and Legal Notices for full disclaimers. Features described below as optional or version-dependent apply only when enabled in the version you use.

1. Information and purposes

Local game data

Streamlord does not require you to create a studio account and does not operate a studio cloud-save server. Game progress, autosaves, manual save slots, local save metadata, in-game founder and company names, language choice, and visual preferences remain on your Android device (subject to Android's standard system backup controls on your device).

Device, network, and service identifiers

The game's integrated Google services may process device model, operating-system and app version, language, IP address, advertising identifiers where available, and technical installation identifiers used to operate those services. Advertising and analytics services may derive an approximate location from network information, such as your IP address.

Google Analytics for Firebase uses an Analytics App Instance ID to associate usage data with an app installation, and Firebase Crashlytics uses its own installation identifier to group crash reports. These identifiers are not your email address, but they can still be associated with usage, crash diagnostics, or advertising activity and are not described here as fully anonymous.

Analytics, crash diagnostics, and app updates

Firebase Analytics collects standard app usage, session, engagement, and device information to help us understand how players use the game and improve stability and balance.

Streamlord configures Firebase Analytics Consent Mode v2 with advertising-related consent signals (AD_STORAGE, AD_USER_DATA, and AD_PERSONALIZATION) denied by default while keeping basic app-usage analytics (ANALYTICS_STORAGE) and Crashlytics enabled. Advertising-related Firebase consent signals are enabled only when both your UMP consent choices and your age-appropriate session policy permit them, and remain denied for under-18, unverified, or restricted sessions.

Firebase Crashlytics processes crash and exception reports containing stack traces, app version, operating-system and device details, and runtime state at the time of a crash so we can diagnose and fix defects.

Google Play In-App Updates checks with Google Play whether a newer version of the game is available and helps coordinate updates on your device.

Age verification signals and age-appropriate experiences

In jurisdictions where age-sharing or age-verification rules apply, Streamlord queries the Google Play Age Signals API at startup (and when returning from Google Play after tapping an in-game age-verification notice) to determine age-appropriate session protections and any applicable parent or guardian approval status (significantChangeStatus).

Streamlord translates these signals in memory only into privacy and safety flags for the current session: restricting ad personalization, capping ad content ratings, disabling third-party ad mediation adapter initialization for under-13 sessions, passing Google's under-age-of-consent tag to UMP when applicable, denying Firebase advertising consent signals, and hiding external social links for under-13 sessions.

Streamlord does not persist raw age bounds, age-verification status, or Play Age Signals install identifiers on your device, in backups, in crash diagnostics, or on any studio server, and never shares raw Play Age Signals data with ad networks or analytics providers for targeting.

In regulated jurisdictions where Google Play reports that age verification or parent/guardian approval is required, Streamlord may show an informational in-game notice directing you to Google Play or Google Family Link while keeping offline core gameplay available.

Rewarded and milestone interstitial advertising

Streamlord includes optional rewarded video ads and, where enabled for eligible sessions, non-intrusive milestone interstitial ads delivered through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK):

  • Optional rewarded video ads: Watching a rewarded ad is strictly voluntary and grants in-game bonuses (such as refreshing the free talent roster, accelerating production or construction, mitigating operational disruptions, or securing emergency bridge financing).
  • Milestone interstitial ads: Shown only at natural gameplay transitions (such as era transitions, year-end recaps, major Tier 3 launches, or awards wins), subject to first-session grace periods, cooldown intervals, and automatic deferral after rewarded ads. Milestone interstitials are permanently removed for users who have made any paid Google Play in-app purchase in Streamlord, and are completely disabled for under-13 and unresolved Play Age Signals sessions.

AdMob initialization and all ad requests wait until both Google Play Age Signals and UMP consent resolve. Coarse location collection is disabled in AdMob requests; ad content is capped at T (Teen) for general and teen sessions and at G (General Audiences) with child-directed treatment and third-party mediation adapter initialization disabled for under-13 sessions; teen (13–17) and mandatory-region unverified sessions receive non-personalized teen-restricted ad treatment. AdMob processes device and advertising identifiers, network information, approximate location, and ad interaction or performance data to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud on ads according to applicable consent, age-appropriate treatment, and device settings.

In-app purchases, consumables, and subscriptions

Where optional in-app purchases are enabled in the version you use — including permanent non-consumable expansions and bundles (Headhunter, Starter Mogul Pack, Executive Expansion, Founder's Complete Edition / Upgrade, and Lifetime Mogul VIP), consumable packs (Ad-Skip Vouchers and Venture Capital Grants), and the optional auto-renewing Streamlord Mogul Pass subscription (monthly or annual) — checkout, payment processing, billing, subscription management, and non-consumable/subscription restoration are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment-card details or Google account password from Google Play.

Streamlord maintains a local entitlement cache so verified purchases remain usable offline and are reconciled with Google Play when online; non-consumable and subscription cache data (entitlement_prefs) is excluded from Android Auto Backup so ownership restores authoritatively from Google Play on reinstall. Separately, Streamlord maintains a local account-scoped consumable wallet (consumable_wallet_prefs) storing unspent voucher and VC Grant counts, processed consumable purchase tokens (solely for idempotent deduplication before acknowledging consumption to Google Play), and your last VIP reward claim timestamp (used strictly on-device to display an informational reminder if your Mogul Pass has gone unused for 14 or more days). This consumable wallet persists across in-game More → Reset Game actions and remains eligible for Android Auto Backup / device transfer when enabled in your Android settings so unspent consumed items survive device restores.

Support communications

If you contact us by email for support or privacy requests, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include so that we can respond.

2. Service providers

Information is processed by the Google services integrated into Streamlord under their own privacy notices:

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for optional rewarded video and milestone interstitial ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and regional or age-appropriate consent management. Streamlord does not currently ship third-party ad mediation networks; if mediation networks are added in a future release, this policy will be updated and mediation adapter initialization remains disabled for under-13 sessions. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps.
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for app usage analytics (including Firebase Analytics Consent Mode defaults and controls) and crash diagnostics. See Firebase privacy and security information.
  • Google Play Billing, Google Play Age Signals, and Google Play In-App Updates for optional in-app purchases, purchase restoration, in-memory age-appropriate session controls in applicable jurisdictions, and app update checks. See the Google Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose relevant information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect users and services from fraud or abuse. A transfer of the studio or its assets may involve relevant information, subject to applicable protections and notice requirements.

3. Advertising and consent choices

Streamlord resolves applicable Google Play Age Signals before requesting consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) in regulated regions (including the EEA, UK, and applicable US states), passing Google's under-age-of-consent tag to UMP when applicable. Ad SDK initialization and ad requests are gated until both Play Age Signals and UMP confirm the appropriate treatment and consent status.

Where required by regional regulations, you can review or update your consent choices at any time inside the game under More → Settings → Ad privacy preferences, and open this privacy policy directly under More → Settings → Privacy policy. If you withdraw consent so that ad requests are no longer permitted, Streamlord clears preloaded ads and stops requesting ads while keeping offline gameplay unaffected. You can also manage or reset your Advertising ID and ad personalization settings through your Android device settings.

Changing advertising choices does not delete local save files, purchase records held by Google Play, or previously collected analytics and crash diagnostics.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Local game data and consumable wallet balances remain on your device until you clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Using the in-game More → Reset Game option deletes local gameplay progress and saves, while unspent consumable wallet balances (Ad-Skip Vouchers and VC Grant credits) and valid Google Play entitlements persist across resets, and non-consumable/subscription purchases remain restorable through your Google Play account. When Android Auto Backup is enabled on your device, unspent consumable wallet data may also be included in your device backup, whereas non-consumable entitlement caches restore directly from Google Play.
  • Google Play Age Signals are processed transiently in memory for the current app session only and discarded immediately after deriving session privacy flags; they are not stored in local saves, backups, crash reports, or studio servers.
  • Analytics and crash diagnostic data are retained according to Firebase's configured settings and lifecycle for measuring usage, troubleshooting, and improving reliability. Aggregated reports may persist separately from individual event records.
  • Google Play purchase and subscription records are managed and retained by Google Play under Google's policies and applicable legal obligations. Resetting a game or uninstalling Streamlord does not cancel an active Google Play subscription; subscriptions are managed and canceled through Google Play Subscriptions.
  • Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

Deleting local data does not automatically erase records already processed by Google or Firebase. Deletion requests are subject to records we can identify and control and to necessary legal, transaction, or security exceptions.

Request deletion or ask about your data: email contact@smartatum.com. Include the game name (Streamlord) and the data or feature concerned. We may ask for limited information to locate the record and verify the request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or purchase tokens. See full deletion instructions.

5. Privacy rights

Depending on your location (including the EEA, UK, and applicable US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal data, object to or restrict processing, opt out of targeted advertising where applicable, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights; we respond within the period required by applicable law.

Where a legal basis is required, processing depends on its purpose: providing requested game features and purchases, legitimate interests in crash diagnostics, reliability, and fraud prevention where permitted, legal obligations, or consent where required. This notice does not replace a consent choice that must be obtained separately.

6. Security, international processing, and children

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and features involved. No system can guarantee complete security. Google and its infrastructure providers may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards.

Streamlord is intended for an audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13; a higher minimum age may apply under local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable age threshold.

Where age-appropriate session controls apply, under-13 sessions automatically have milestone interstitial ads completely disabled and receive child-safe G-rated AdMob-only treatment with ad personalization, third-party mediation adapter initialization, Firebase advertising signals, and external social links disabled and UMP tagged for under-age of consent; teen (13–17) sessions receive non-personalized T-rated ad treatment with Firebase advertising signals denied, and mandatory-region unverified sessions keep milestone interstitials disabled until Play Age Signals resolves. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to these requirements, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We update this policy when data practices or services change and show the revised date on this page. The current official policy is hosted on smartatum.com. See our Terms of Use and Legal Notices and studio privacy overview for additional legal, website, and product information.

Streamlord Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Smart Atum Studio is an independent game studio operated by Ramy Tawfik ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy describes how information is handled in Streamlord for Android, package com.smartatum.streamlord.

Streamlord is an offline-capable media-business entertainment simulation. Founder names, studio names, talent rosters, revenues, and financial figures created in gameplay are fictional in-game content, not real-world personal or financial records. Features described below as optional or version-dependent apply only when enabled in the version you use.

1. Information and purposes

Local game data

Streamlord does not require you to create a studio account and does not operate a studio cloud-save server. Game progress, autosaves, manual save slots, local save metadata, in-game founder and company names, language choice, and visual preferences remain on your Android device (subject to Android's standard system backup controls on your device).

Device, network, and service identifiers

The game's integrated Google services may process device model, operating-system and app version, language, IP address, advertising identifiers where available, and technical installation identifiers used to operate those services. Advertising and analytics services may derive an approximate location from network information, such as your IP address.

Google Analytics for Firebase uses an Analytics App Instance ID to associate usage data with an app installation, and Firebase Crashlytics uses its own installation identifier to group crash reports. These identifiers are not your email address, but they can still be associated with usage, crash diagnostics, or advertising activity and are not described here as fully anonymous.

Analytics, crash diagnostics, and app updates

Firebase Analytics collects standard app usage, session, engagement, and device information to help us understand how players use the game and improve stability and balance.

Firebase Crashlytics processes crash and exception reports containing stack traces, app version, operating-system and device details, and runtime state at the time of a crash so we can diagnose and fix defects.

Google Play In-App Updates checks with Google Play whether a newer version of the game is available and helps coordinate updates on your device.

Rewarded advertising

Streamlord includes optional rewarded video ads delivered through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK). Watching an ad is voluntary and grants in-game bonuses (such as refreshing the free talent roster, boosting production capacity, or extending bankruptcy grace weeks). AdMob processes device and advertising identifiers, network information, approximate location, and ad interaction or performance data to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud on ads according to applicable consent and device settings.

In-app purchases

Where optional one-time in-app purchases are enabled in the version you use (such as unlocking the Headhunter feature), purchases, payment processing, and entitlement restoration on reinstall or new devices are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive your payment-card details or Google account password from Google Play. Local entitlement cache data may be stored on your device so valid purchases remain available during offline play and are reconciled with Google Play when online.

Support communications

If you contact us by email for support or privacy requests, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include so that we can respond.

2. Service providers

Information is processed by the Google services integrated into Streamlord under their own privacy notices:

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for optional rewarded ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and regional consent management. Streamlord does not currently use third-party ad mediation networks. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps.
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for app usage analytics and crash diagnostics. See Firebase privacy and security information.
  • Google Play Billing and Google Play In-App Updates for optional in-app purchases, purchase restoration, and app update checks. See the Google Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose relevant information when required by law, to respond to a valid legal request, or to protect users and services from fraud or abuse. A transfer of the studio or its assets may involve relevant information, subject to applicable protections and notice requirements.

3. Advertising and consent choices

Streamlord uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain applicable advertising consent choices in regulated regions (including the EEA, UK, and applicable US states). Ad SDK initialization and ad requests are gated until UMP confirms that ad requests are permitted.

Where required by regional regulations, you can review or update your consent choices at any time inside the game under More → Settings → Ad privacy preferences. If you withdraw consent so that ad requests are no longer permitted, Streamlord clears preloaded ads and stops requesting ads while keeping offline gameplay unaffected. You can also manage or reset your Advertising ID and ad personalization settings through your Android device settings.

Changing advertising choices does not delete local save files, purchase records held by Google Play, or previously collected analytics and crash diagnostics.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Local game data remains on your device until you use the in-game More → Reset Game option, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Note that resetting a game removes local gameplay progress and saves, while valid Google Play purchases remain restorable through your Google Play account.
  • Analytics and crash diagnostic data are retained according to Firebase's configured settings and lifecycle for measuring usage, troubleshooting, and improving reliability. Aggregated reports may persist separately from individual event records.
  • Google Play purchase records are managed and retained by Google Play under Google's policies and applicable legal obligations.
  • Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

Deleting local data does not automatically erase records already processed by Google or Firebase. Deletion requests are subject to records we can identify and control and to necessary legal, transaction, or security exceptions.

Request deletion or ask about your data: email smartatum@gmail.com. Include the game name (Streamlord) and the data or feature concerned. We may ask for limited information to locate the record and verify the request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or purchase tokens. See full deletion instructions.

5. Privacy rights

Depending on your location (including the EEA, UK, and applicable US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal data, object to or restrict processing, opt out of targeted advertising where applicable, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights; we respond within the period required by applicable law.

Where a legal basis is required, processing depends on its purpose: providing requested game features and purchases, legitimate interests in crash diagnostics, reliability, and fraud prevention where permitted, legal obligations, or consent where required. This notice does not replace a consent choice that must be obtained separately.

6. Security, international processing, and children

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and features involved. No system can guarantee complete security. Google and its infrastructure providers may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards.

Streamlord is not directed to children under 13; a higher minimum age may apply under local law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable age threshold. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to these requirements, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

7. Contact and changes

We update this policy when data practices or services change and show the revised date on this page. The current official policy is hosted on smartatum.com. See our studio privacy overview for website and other product information.

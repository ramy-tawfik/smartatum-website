About Smart Atum

Creation is a strategy.

Smart Atum is an independent game studio founded by solo developer Ramy Tawfik.

Inspired by Atum, the ancient Egyptian creator god, Smart Atum builds games about ambition, systems, and the choices that shape a future.

We make accessible games with room to think—whether you are making your first move on Android or settling in for a long session on PC.
